Christmas Markets in Zurich 2026

Every Christmas market in Zurich 2026 with dates, locations and opening hours, including the brand-new Bellevue Noël at Sechseläutenplatz.

By Laura Wick and Eva Wick · 25 October 2026 · 1 min read

From 19 November, Zurich turns into a city of Christmas markets. Here are all the key markets at a glance, including the new Bellevue Noël, held for the first time in 2026.

MarketDates
Bellevue Noël, Sechseläutenplatz19 Nov – 23 Dec
Main station19 Nov – 24 Dec
Dörfli, Old Town19 Nov – 23 Dec
Weihnachtsallee, Europaallee19 Nov – 23 Dec
Münsterhof19 Nov – 24 Dec
Singing Christmas Tree, Werdmühleplatz19 Nov – 23 Dec

Our tip

Book a fondue chalet early, and combine your visit with a stroll under the Lucy lights on Bahnhofstrasse.

Information as of October 2026, without guarantee.

FAQ

When do the Zurich Christmas markets open in 2026?

Most markets open on 19 November 2026 and run until 23 or 24 December.

Which Christmas market in Zurich is indoors?

The market at Zurich main station is held inside the station hall and is protected from the rain.
