Corippo Albergo Diffuso in Ticino

A night in one of Switzerland's smallest villages: simple rooms in old stone rustici, an osteria cooking from the valley and deep calm.

By Eva Wick · 29 October 2026 · 1 min read

Disclosure: We were invited for one night. We were not paid. This is our honest opinion.

Corippo sits in the heart of the Verzasca Valley and was long Switzerland’s smallest municipality. To bring the village back to life, it became a hotel, with rooms spread across its old stone rustici.

The rooms: simple, full of charm

Don’t expect luxury. Instead you sleep in the middle of a historic village, surrounded by nature and silence.

Breakfast: thoughtful and lovely

Every guest gets their own tray with homemade granola. We took a few jars home with us.

Our verdict

Calm, nature, fresh cooking and a hotel with real character: special and enchanting.

FAQ

What is an albergo diffuso?

A scattered hotel: the rooms are spread across historic houses throughout an entire village.

Can you eat at Corippo Albergo Diffuso without staying overnight?

Yes, the osteria takes table reservations. Please book ahead.
